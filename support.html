Common questions

  • My video won't analyze / is stuck. Make sure the clip is shot from the side (phone on a tripod 3–5 m away, facing your side) and the player is fully in frame. If it still stalls, email us the approximate upload time and we'll look it up.
  • The scores or shot detection look wrong. Tell us which session and which shot — we use real reports to improve the model.
  • Billing / subscription. Pro unlocks the Tina AI chat coach and multiple player profiles. Manage or cancel anytime from your device's App Store subscription settings (iOS) or the billing portal on the web.
  • Delete my account / data. Email us from your account's address and we'll remove your account and all associated data within 30 days.
